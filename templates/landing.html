About the System

Built for ESSU-Guiuan students under academic pressure. Study tips, motivation, and practical guidance in simple words. Purpose: stress, burnout, thesis, exams, and grades. Who can use it: ESSU-Guiuan students with an account. Languages: English, Tagalog, and Waray. Free for the campus.

How to Use
